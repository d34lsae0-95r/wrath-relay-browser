Run a relay?

This browser can act as a relay node: it forwards encrypted blobs for the privacy mesh. This keeps the network alive when servers go down.

What the relay does:
  • Forwards opaque encrypted blobs (max 64KB each, max 100/day).
  • Never reads them. Never knows sender or destination. Can't — they're encrypted.
  • Uses a little bandwidth. Nothing else.
What it NEVER does:
  • Never touches your files. Never runs programs. Never watches you browse.
  • Never persists past uninstall. One click off, right here, anytime.
  • Your own machine is never a target — the relay serves others, never infects self.

The relay source is public: relay.py in this repo. Read it. It can't do what it can't do.

Default: OFF. Uninstall removes everything in ~/.wrath-relay.
